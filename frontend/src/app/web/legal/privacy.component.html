Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-25

This describes what ResuRank actually stores and why, in plain terms rather than boilerplate. It is not a substitute for professional legal advice.

What never leaves your browser

Your resume PDF is parsed locally in your browser. The file itself is never uploaded. Match scoring — the embedding model and the term comparison — also runs entirely in your browser, not on our server.

What we store

To run the service, we store, tied to your account:

  • Your email address and, if you provide one, your name.
  • Your password as an argon2id hash — we never store or can see your actual password.
  • The extracted text of resumes you upload, plus the list of terms derived from it (not the PDF itself).
  • Job descriptions and titles you evaluate, and the resulting match scores, so you have a history to look back on.
  • Your scoring preferences (excluded words, term boosts, missing-keyword and preference settings).
  • Session identifiers (as one-way hashes) so you can stay signed in, and short-lived tokens for email verification and password resets.

Email

We send email only for account actions you take: verifying your address, resetting your password, and notifying you of security-relevant changes (password or email changed). We don't send marketing email and don't share your address with anyone for that purpose.

What we don't do

No third-party analytics, ad trackers, or behavioral tracking of any kind run on this site. We don't sell your data or share it with third parties, except the infrastructure needed to run the service itself (hosting and email delivery), and never for advertising.

Your controls

From the Account screen you can export a full JSON copy of everything above at any time, or permanently delete your account — which immediately removes your resumes, settings, and scoring history, and cannot be undone.
